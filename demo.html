What it shows

  • 0:20 – HM AppLock open, one app (Clock) locked, protection on.
  • 0:32 – The ongoing “HM AppLock is protecting your apps” notification of the foreground service.
  • 0:42 – Opening the locked app: the lock screen appears on top of it; the PIN opens it.
  • 1:40 – Settings → Turbo mode: the in-app prominent disclosure for the Accessibility Service API, shown before anything is enabled, with “No thanks” and “Agree and continue”.
  • 1:50 – After consent, the user turns on “HM AppLock Turbo” in Android Accessibility settings.
  • 2:15 – With Turbo on, the lock screen appears as soon as the locked app is opened.
