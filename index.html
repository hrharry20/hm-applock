HM AppLock

Lock any app on your Android phone or tablet with a PIN, pattern, password, fingerprint or face.

Features

  • Lock any app: chats, gallery, payments, settings
  • PIN, pattern or password, plus fingerprint and face unlock
  • Intruder selfie after repeated wrong attempts
  • Uninstall protection
  • Crash disguise, shuffled keypad, invisible pattern
  • Six themes
  • No ads, no accounts, no internet access

Help

Apps are not being locked. Open HM AppLock and allow “Usage access” and “Display over other apps”. On some phones also allow the app to auto-start and set its battery use to “Not optimised”.

I forgot my PIN or pattern. Tap “Forgot” on the HM AppLock lock screen and answer your recovery question. Set one in Settings → Recovery question.

I want to uninstall. Open HM AppLock → Settings → switch off “Uninstall protection”, then uninstall as usual.

Privacy

HM AppLock collects no data and cannot access the internet. Read the privacy policy.
